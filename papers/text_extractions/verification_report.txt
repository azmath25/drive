Editorial and mathematical flags

1. F1 — Incorrect submersion wording (repaired). The source calls p 7→ N (p) · u a
   submersion; a real-valued smooth function on a compact surface always has critical
   points. Only the zero level needs to be regular for almost all views.

2. F2 — Unjustified transversality/stratification claim (repaired). The source ap-
   peals to a codimension-one set without constructing the relevant map or proving the
   stratification. Sard’s theorem on the incidence manifold is sufficient.

3. F3 — Missing coarea Jacobian calculation (repaired). On M the relevant Jaco-
   bians are J2 πp = (1 + |Bu|2 )−1/2 and J2 πu = |Bu|(1 + |Bu|2 )−1/2 .

4. F4 — Degenerate ellipse ambiguity (repaired). The ellipse perimeter is inter-
   preted as the length of its parameterized image, counting a collapsed segment twice.

5. F5 — Visibility terminology (qualified). Spatial rim length, projected rim length
   with multiplicity, and visible image silhouette length are not equal in general. The
   corrected paper explicitly distinguishes them.

6. F6 — Literature and priority (open). Existing work defines and studies contour
   generators and apparent contours. A targeted search did not verify a previous state-
   ment of these exact averaged formulas, but absence of a search hit is not evidence
   of novelty.

7. F7 — Added results and author attribution (open). Equality classification, lower
   comparison, and projected identity were developed during this review. They should
   not be silently presented as content of the original note or submitted without autho-
   rial review.

8. F8 — Hypothesis scope (qualified). The proof covers compact smooth oriented
   surfaces without boundary in R3 . A surface with boundary or an unbounded surface
   would require separate hypotheses or boundary/integrability provisions.
